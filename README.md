# Customer Sales Performance Analysis and Business Insights
## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing retail sales data to identify business trends, evaluate product performance, understand customer behaviour, and improve profitability.
The analysis is performed using the Sample Superstore Dataset.
## Business Objectives

- Identify the highest revenue-generating product categories.
- Analyse sales and profit performance across regions.
- Identify valuable customer segments.
- Understand monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Analyse the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Category Performance
- Technology generates the highest sales and profit.
- Furniture generates good sales but comparatively low profit.
- Office Supplies contributes significant profit.

### 2. Regional Performance
- West is the highest-performing region in sales and profit.
- Central requires improvement in profitability.
- South has the lowest regional sales.

### 3. Customer Insights
- Consumer is the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 50% of sales.
- Sean Miller is the highest revenue-generating customer in the reported analysis.
- Tamara Chand is the highest profit-generating customer.

### 4. Sales Trends
- November and December are the peak sales months.
- January and February record the lowest sales.
- Yearly sales show an overall increasing trend in the analysed period.

### 5. Profitability Analysis
- Higher discounts are associated with lower profit.
- Some Furniture products generate losses.
- Technology and Office Supplies show stronger profit performance.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus on high-profit Technology products.
2. Review Furniture pricing and discount strategies.
3. Reduce unnecessary discounts.
4. Improve marketing performance in Central and South regions.
5. Focus on customer retention and high-value customers.
6. Plan promotional campaigns before peak sales months.
